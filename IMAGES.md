Notes:

- Keep the hero photo as your real portrait and keep the fallback initials to RG.
- Use the same file names above in the public folder when you are ready to replace the placeholders.
- Keep all real image files compressed and under 300 KB for faster page loads.
- Do not reuse the profile photo in other slots.
